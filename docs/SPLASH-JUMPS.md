# Distinct Jump / Boost and splash chains

Space / BOOST always performs an underwater dash. J, Shift or JUMP launches at the swimmer's current position; no up input or marked launch point is required. Existing energy requirement, powered animations and one-use boss leap are retained. Invisible route anchors remain only for sky collectible distribution and the existing boss leap distance; they no longer draw or grant faster recharge.

A fresh Jump press within 220 ms before water contact or 240 ms afterward schedules a bounce. Sarah dips 43 world units underwater over 160 ms, then launches in a combo flip. The first, second and subsequent chained bounces award 100, 200 and 300 points, with up to +3 treasure multiplier levels (combined cap ×8). The multiplier lasts four seconds after the bounce. Timing rewards use momentum rather than extra energy; normal launches still cost 40% boost. Holding Jump cannot queue repeats. Pause freezes simulation; level loads and retries reset chains.

The Jump button glows during the timing window. Tutorial and desktop footer now teach J / JUMP and separate Space / BOOST. Existing stage-transition portals remain unchanged.

Checks: splash-jump, open-jumps, mobile-v55, tutorial, controls, conversation and Rana encounter suites pass. Physical device testing remains outstanding.
